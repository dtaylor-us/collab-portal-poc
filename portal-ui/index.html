About this POC

One collaboration experience, separate systems of record

The Planning Results Portal presents a business-focused experience over the DPP Collaboration API. PostgreSQL remains authoritative for review status, corrections, comments, and dispositions. Camunda coordinates long-running tasks, reminders, routing, retries, incidents, and rework.

Business record

Review identity, collaboration history, and decisions stay in the application data model.

Workflow

Camunda manages durable human work and process coordination without becoming the business database.

Recovery

Idempotency keys allow safe retry when application state commits but workflow advancement fails.
